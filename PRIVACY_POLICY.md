# Privacy Policy — vasilena-markova-yoga-vibe

**Last updated:** February 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application **vasilena-markova-yoga-vibe** (Vasilena Markova Yoga), available on Android and other platforms.

## 1. Who we are

This app is made for **Vasilena Markova**, yoga teacher. The app helps users practice yoga at home with courses and poses (asanas). For technical and hosting purposes, the app uses services provided by Google (Firebase) and Expo.

## 2. Information we collect

- **If you use the app without an account (guest):**  
  We may store a push notification token (if you allow notifications) so we can send you updates (e.g. new courses). This is stored in a secure database and is not used to identify you personally.

- **If you create an account:**  
  We collect and store:
  - **Email address** and **name** (for your account).
  - **Profile photo** (optional), if you set one — it is stored in a secure cloud storage.
  - **Progress data** — which asanas and courses you have completed, so we can show your progress when you log in on the same account.
  - **Push notification token** — so we can send you notifications (e.g. when new courses are added).

We do **not** sell your data to third parties.

## 3. How we use your information

- To provide and improve the app (courses, progress, profile).
- To send push notifications about new content, if you have allowed notifications.
- To keep your account secure and allow you to sign in on your devices.

## 4. Data storage and security

- Data is stored using **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Storage).  
- We use industry-standard security practices.  
- You can delete your account or ask us to delete your data (see “Contact” below).

## 5. Your rights

You can:
- Use the app as a guest without creating an account.
- Refuse or disable push notifications at any time.
- Request access to or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be changed when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
